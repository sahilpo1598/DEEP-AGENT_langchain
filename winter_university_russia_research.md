# Winter University in Russia — Research Report

*Compiled research on winter university / winter school programs in Russia for international (and Russian) students.*

---

## 1. Overview

"Winter University" in Russia refers to two related but distinct things:

1. **The Winter University of Engineering Sciences** — a flagship, largely free international project run by leading Russian technical universities (SPbPU, Samara University, etc.), typically held in **late November–early December**. It is the winter counterpart to the "Summer University" project.
2. **A broad ecosystem of winter schools / short-term winter programs** hosted by individual universities (JINR, ITMO, ETU "LETI", SPbU, UrFU, TSU, Skoltech) covering research internships, engineering, AI, and Russian-language/cultural study.

Most programs target **final-year bachelor's / master's / PhD students**, are delivered in **English and/or Russian**, and many are **fully or partially funded** for selected international participants.

---

## 2. The Winter University of Engineering Sciences (flagship project)

- **Official site:** https://winteruniversity.ru/en
- **Format:** International educational + project + cultural program for engineering students.
- **Recent edition (2025):** Theme **"Artificial Intelligence and Digital Technologies"**, held **24 November – 7 December 2025**, with **200+ third-/fourth-year undergraduate engineering students from 44 universities** across Russia, Europe, Asia, and Africa.
- **Structure:** Each participant's program has three parts:
  1. **Educational module** (lectures, specialised sessions)
  2. **Project activity** (real industry tasks, mentorship)
  3. **Cultural-excursion part** (guided tours, workshops, intercultural events)
- **Tracks (2025 example):** Three engineering tracks under the AI theme — including a "Technologist" track. For **2026**, SPbPU's concept focuses on applying AI tools (neural networks) to design, modelling, and optimisation within participants' own engineering projects — not just AI theory.
- **Participating / host universities** (2025–2026):
  - **Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University (SPbPU)** — main/host university
  - **Samara University** — e.g., "Design and Construction of Modern Unmanned Aircraft Systems"
  - Others coordinated through winteruniversity.ru
- **Eligibility:** Open to **final-year bachelor's (3rd–4th year) and final-year specialist's (4th–5th year)** students enrolled at Russian and foreign universities; participants should be ready for an **intensive academic program**.
- **Language:** Sessions offered **in both Russian and English**.
- **Cost:** Largely **free** for selected participants (educational program + stay typically covered by organizers; travel arrangements vary).
- **Application timeline:** Applications generally open **mid-August** and close **late September–mid-October**.
  - Example dates reported: application period **17 Aug – 4 Oct 2026**; one source lists a deadline of **11 October 2026** (deadlines vary by year/edition — always verify on the official site).

### SPbPU — "Winter University" host details
- Site: https://english.spbstu.ru/international/collaboration/international-initiatives/winter-university-of-engineering-sciences
- Delivered by SPbPU's **Advanced Engineering School "Digital Engineering"** and institutes such as:
  - National Technology Initiative Centre "New Production Technologies"
  - Engineering Centre "Centre for Computational Engineering" (CompMechLab®)
  - Centre for Continuing Professional Education
  - Higher School of Advanced Digital Technologies
- 2026 theme: synergy of engineering thinking and advanced technologies; practical implementation of AI across data analysis, forecasting, classification, decision support, process optimisation, and digital services.

## 3. JINR START Winter Program (research internship — fully funded)

- **Host:** Joint Institute for Nuclear Research (JINR), **Dubna, Russia** — an international intergovernmental research center.
- **Format:** Fully funded **6–8 week** winter research program/internship at JINR.
- **Eligibility:** 4th/5th-year undergraduates, Master's students, and **first-year PhD students** in relevant scientific fields. Open to **all nationalities**.
- **Coverage (fully funded):** round-trip travel reimbursement (to Dubna and back), accommodation in Dubna, meals/monthly allowance.
- **Language of work:** English; **no IELTS required** per several listing sources.
- **Timeline:** Applications typically close **30 October** for the winter intake (e.g., deadline 30 Oct 2025 for the 2026 program). Program generally runs February onwards.
- **Note:** JINR also runs a **Summer Student Program** (similar funded format).
- **Where to verify:** JINR official site (jinr.ru) — third-party scholarship aggregators list it widely, so confirm details at the source.

## 4. University winter schools (selected examples)

### ITMO University (St. Petersburg)
- **Summer and Winter Schools 2026** open to international partner-university students.
- Focus areas: IT, AI, digital economy, entrepreneurship/startups, research-to-business.
- **Audience:** students in IT/AI/digital economy, aspiring founders, researchers, startup teams.
- Typically modest fee (partner listings cite ~**USD 500/student** for some tracks); English proficiency ~**B2**.

### ETU "LETI" — St. Petersburg Electrotechnical University
- **International Winter Schools** in St. Petersburg — multiple themed schools (six in a recent edition) with 70+ international students.
- Emphasis on **professional development + Russian culture**; classes in classrooms/labs plus excursions around St. Petersburg and suburbs.
- Site: https://etu.ru/en/study/international-winter-schools

### St. Petersburg State University (SPbU)
- **Winter School of Russian as a Foreign Language** — intensive ~2-week Russian language + culture program.
- Site: https://english.spbu.ru/admission/programms/additional-educational-programmes/winter-school-russian-foreign-language

### Ural Federal University (UrFU), Yekaterinburg
- Runs the **"Summer University"** project (July; 3rd/4th-year bachelor's students; travel, accommodation, meals, transfers covered by organizers). Uses the same platform as the Winter University.
- Site: https://wintersummeruni.urfu.ru/en ; https://summeruniversity.ru/en/program/urfu

### Skoltech (Moscow)
- International technological university (English-medium); has run themed winter schools, e.g., a **Life Sciences Winter School** (3 days; final-year students from leading Russian universities).
- Site: https://skoltech.ru/en

### Tomsk State University (TSU)
- Offers **Russian as a Foreign Language** winter/summer schools; course costs vary (~RUB 20,000–30,000 by credits).

## 5. Russian language & cultural winter schools

- **SPbU** — 2-week Winter School of Russian as a Foreign Language.
- **TSU (Tomsk)** — Russian language winter/summer schools.
- **Moscow + Yekaterinburg combined programs** — e.g., "Russian Language Winter Schools and the New Year Festival" packages (bundling educational fee + accommodation + meals; overseas listings cite ~€1,400 total for some packages).
- Costs typically split into: **educational fee + accommodation/meals + cultural program**.

## 6. Common eligibility & application notes

| Item | Typical requirement |
|---|---|
| Study level | Final-year bachelor's / specialist, master's, or PhD |
| Language | English (B2+) and/or Russian |
| Age | 18+ (varies) |
| Documents | Passport, transcript, CV, motivation letter, sometimes recommendation |
| Cost | Flagship Winter University largely free; JINR fully funded; university winter schools often modest fee |
| Deadlines | Winter University: ~Aug–Oct; JINR: ~30 Oct; individual schools vary |

---

## 7. Key takeaways

- The **"Winter University of Engineering Sciences"** is Russia's flagship winter program — free, AI/engineering-focused, hosted primarily by **SPbPU** with partner universities, held late Nov–early Dec, applications due ~Oct.
- **JINR START** is the strongest **fully funded** option for science research students (nuclear/physics/related), 6–8 weeks in Dubna.
- Many top universities (**ITMO, ETU "LETI", SPbU, Skoltech, TSU**) run their own winter schools — some academic/technical, some Russian-language/cultural.
- Programs frequently run in **both Russian and English**, which is a distinguishing feature for engineering tracks.
